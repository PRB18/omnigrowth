How borders are loaded and displayed

This component is responsible for loading coordinate bounds of Indian states and districts dynamically, rendering them on a standard canvas, and processing click events.

Simple Analogy
"Imagine an digital coloring book of India. The coloring pages are coordinate files (GeoJSON) and the artist drawing them live onto your screen when you zoom in is the Map logic director (useMapLogic)."
client/public/geodata/*.geojson

Stores precise physical coordinate markers detailing boundaries of all Indian states.

client/src/hooks/useMapLogic.ts

The brains of the map. Controls panning, zooming, styling, and selected districts.

client/src/pages/DashboardUI.tsx

The visual shell displaying the Leaflet map and side detailed cards.

How MSP and market indices are resolved

This module handles pre-populated local soil data, seasonal crops (Kharif/Rabi), and maps them against Government floor prices (Minimum Support Prices).

Simple Analogy
"Think of this as a digital ledger. It maintains detailed index cards for each Indian district. When you select a crop, the calculator checks the current market prices against government support targets."
client/src/lib/data.ts

Contains structured local dictionaries for all states, capitals, crops, and soils.

client/src/lib/priceResolve.ts

The calculation center resolving live market indices vs target Minimum Support Prices.

client/src/pages/Prices.tsx

The dedicated user dashboard visualizing price metrics with rich, clear charts.

How security and records are protected

The system uses safe database protocols, enforcing Row Level Security rules on all database tables and preventing unauthorized credentials from accessing records.

Simple Analogy
"Imagine a secure gym locker room. Every person has their own private locker. Our security settings (Row Level Security) act as padlock keys, ensuring only YOU can see or edit your contents."
client/src/lib/supabase.ts

Enables secure client-side connections to database systems, hiding private credentials.

client/src/lib/auth.ts

Provides session tracking, registration, login processes, and account verification.

supabase/migrations/*.sql

Database-level policies ensuring no external requests can bypass safety rules.

How market prices are retrieved and loaded

Automatic workers sync daily price indexes from national feeds (like Agmarknet) and store them cleanly so the front-end page remains ultra-fast.

Simple Analogy
"Think of this as a night courier. It automatically visits government portals while you sleep, retrieves the latest crop index updates, and writes them cleanly into our database."
client/scripts/sync-prices.mjs

Active javascript scraper retrieving recent national bulletins and syncing them.

DATA_SYNC.md

The clear step-by-step playbook detailing how to execute price updates safely.
